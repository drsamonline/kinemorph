# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Critique the work, not the person.

## Expected

- Technical disagreement is welcome and useful. Bring evidence: a reproduction, a measurement,
  a link.
- Give people room to be wrong and to change their mind.
- Keep reviews about the code.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or sustained disruption in issues, pull requests or discussions.
- Discriminatory comments about any person or group.
- Publishing someone's private information.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block accounts that break these
rules. Report a problem through a private security advisory or by contacting the maintainer
listed in `CITATION.cff`.
